1. Getting Started: Signup, Login & Store Setup

1.1 Registering a New Merchant Account

Any business owner can register and launch their cloud ERP workspace within 60 seconds:

1 Navigate to Signup: Open your browser and go to https://smartvyapar.vercel.app/signup.
2 Enter Business Information: Provide your Business Name, Owner Full Name, Mobile Number, Email Address, and Secure Password.
3 Select Business Classification: Choose from Automobile / Auto Spares, Electrical & Electronics, Retail Supermarket, Pharmacy & Healthcare, or Restaurant / Food.
4 Automatic Workspace Provisioning: The system immediately initializes your tenant database, default Chart of Accounts, system accounts, and security keys.

1.2 Logging In to Your Workspace

Visit /login and sign in using your registered credentials or the pre-loaded Evaluation Account (demo@smartvyapar.app / SmartVyapar@2026).

1.3 Configuring Store Profile & Statutory GST Details

Go to Settings (Gear Icon in left navigation):

  • GSTIN: Enter your 15-character statutory GSTIN (e.g., 32AAAAA0000A1Z5). SmartVyapar automatically extracts your 2-digit Home State Code.
  • UPI VPA ID: Enter your shop's UPI address (e.g. mybusiness@icici). This dynamically generates payment QR codes on bills.
  • Store Logo & Header: Upload your branding logo to be printed on receipts and WhatsApp PDF bills.
  • Composition Scheme: Toggle ON if you are registered under the GST Composition Scheme (1% or 5% flat rate).

2. Inventory, Batches & Purchase Inward (GRN)

2.1 Products, HSN Codes & Multiple Units

SmartVyapar supports standard retail SKUs, batch-managed items, and service lines:

  • Barcode / SKU: Compatible with all standard 1D/2D USB and Bluetooth barcode scanners.
  • HSN / SAC Code: 4, 6, or 8-digit statutory HSN code for automated Table 12 GSTR-1 summaries.
  • GST Tax Slabs: 0% (Exempt), 5%, 12%, 18%, or 28%. The system automatically bifurcates CGST + SGST (Intra-State) or IGST (Inter-State).
  • DGFT Statutory UQC: Units mapped to official standards: PCS, NOS, KGS, LTR, BOX, CTN, MTR.

2.2 Batch & Expiry Management

For items requiring expiration monitoring (e.g. engine oils, batteries, lubricants, pharmaceuticals):

  • Track unique Batch Numbers with separate Manufacturing Date and Expiry Date.
  • System displays automatic color-coded badges: 🟢 Fresh, 🟡 Expiring within 60 days, 🔴 Expired.
  • Each batch maintains its own distinct Cost Price, MRP, and Selling Price.

2.3 World-Standard Full-Screen Purchase Inward & GRN Modal

Navigate to Inventory & Purchases (/inventory/purchase) and click "+ New Inward Bill / GRN":

1 Select Supplier: Type supplier name in the instant search box. If new, click "+ Quick Add Supplier" to register with GSTIN, Phone, and State Code.
2 Godown Allocation: Select target warehouse (Main Godown, Counter Rack, Transit Store).
3 Barcode Scanning / Item Addition: Scan item barcode or search by name. Press Alt+A to add new rows.
4 Bidirectional Sales Margin Engine:
  • Enter Cost Price & Selling Price → Margin % calculates automatically.
  • Enter Margin % → Selling Price updates instantly.
  • Bulk Strategy Toolbar: Click presets (15%, 20%, 25%, 30%, 35%) or enter custom margin to apply across all bill items in 1-click.
5 Save & Confirm Inward: Press Ctrl+S. The system generates statutory GRN number (GRN-2026-XXXX), posts double-entry journal vouchers to Trade Payables (A/c 2000), updates stock in the godown, and claims Input Tax Credit (ITC).

2.4 Printing GRN Slips & Barcode Sticker Labels

  • 🖨️ Print GRN Slip: Official inward audit slip showing received quantities, batch numbers, supplier invoice reference, and inspector sign-off.
  • 🏷️ Print Barcode Stickers: Generates high-density Code128 barcode stickers ready for standard thermal label printers (TVS, TSC, Zebra) with Product Name, MRP, Our Price, and SKU.

3.2 Offline-First POS Engine (IndexedDB Local Queue)

Zero Downtime Internet Outages: If your store broadband or Wi-Fi drops during a busy evening rush, SmartVyapar automatically detects offline status via its native IndexedDB engine.
  • The cashier can continue scanning barcodes and issuing invoices without interruption.
  • Bills are queued locally with official offline sequence OFF-YYYYMMDD-XXXX.
  • As soon as the internet reconnects, the system automatically flushes queued bills to the cloud server with zero duplicate entries.

3.3 Silent Thermal ESC/POS Direct Hardware Driver

SmartVyapar supports standard 2-Inch (58mm) and 3-Inch (80mm) thermal roll printers:

  • Standard Browser Print (Enter): Prints via Windows printer dialog.
  • ⚡ Silent Print (Direct USB/Serial): Uses the built-in EPSON ESC/POS binary engine via WebSerial. Directly triggers thermal printing, cutter blade, and cash drawer kick without any browser popups.
  • Dynamic UPI QR on Receipt: Prints a live UPI QR code containing the exact bill amount. Customers scan with PhonePe, GPay, or Paytm to pay instantly.

4.1 WhatsApp Cloud Invoicing & 2-Way Bot

Located in Accounting & Reports (/accounting/reports):

  • Profit & Loss (P&L): Gross sales, Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), Gross Profit %, Operating Expenses, and Net Profit.

5. Automated Bank Reconciliation (BRS) & Statutory GST Filing

5.1 Automated Bank Reconciliation Statement (BRS)

Navigate to Accounting → Bank Reconciliation (BRS) (/accounting/reconciliation):

1 Select Bank Account Ledger: Choose your bank account (e.g. A/c 1001 HDFC Current Account).
2 Upload Bank Statement CSV: Export your statement from HDFC, SBI, ICICI, or Axis NetBanking and drag-and-drop into the upload zone.
3 Auto-Reconcile: Click "Run Automated Reconciliation". The AI matching algorithm compares dates within $\pm 3$ days, matches reference/UTR numbers, and pairs Bank Debits with Book Credits.
4 Review Variance: View matched transactions (Green) and items pending clearance (Amber). Identifies bank charges or interest not yet booked in your ledger.

5.2 Official GST Filing (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, e-Invoice & e-Way Bill)

SmartVyapar conforms 100% to the official CBIC and GSTN portal schema specifications:

5.3 How to File to Government GST Portal

Go to Invoices Register (/invoices):

  • Method 1: Portal Upload JSON (95% of SMEs & CAs): Click "Portal JSON (GSTR-1)". Upload the downloaded ${GSTIN}_GSTR1_${MMYYYY}.json file to gst.gov.in → Returns Dashboard → Prepare Offline → Upload. Tables 4, 7, 9B, 12, and 13 auto-populate within seconds!
  • Method 2: ⚡ 1-Click Direct File (GSP Gateway): Click "⚡ 1-Click Direct File (GSP)". SmartVyapar authenticates with the GSP/NIC API gateway, submits the return, and generates an official 15-character statutory ARN confirmation on your screen.
