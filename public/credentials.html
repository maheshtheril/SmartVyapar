Step-by-Step Testing Guide

🛒 1. Retail / Supermarket

  • Open POS billing counter.
  • Scan/enter barcode 890103000001 (Basmati Rice).
  • Test cash drawer float and change calculation.
  • Print bill on default customer standard A4 format.

🍽️ 2. Restaurant & Cafe

  • Open Table View: Click table T-01 or AC-1.
  • Add Special Malabar Dum Biryani and fire KOT.
  • Check stock: Raw Chicken & Rice auto-deplete per BOM.
  • Settle bill via UPI QR or Split bill.

🚗 3. Automobile & Garage

  • Open Job Cards: Inspect JC-2026-0001.
  • Check linked vehicle KL-07-CB-5544 (Creta).
  • Bill SAC 998714 Labour + Ceramic Brake Pad parts.
  • Generate final GST Tax invoice.

📦 4. Wholesale & Distribution

  • Open Warehouses: Check WH-CENTRAL & VAN-01.
  • Dispatch stock transfer to mobile sales van.
  • Bill Sunflower Oil in BOX (12 PCS) or PCS.
  • Verify wholesale 15% discount price list.

💊 5. Pharmacy & Healthcare

  • Bill Paracetamol 650mg tabs.
  • Notice batch alert: Fresh vs Near-Expiry (Nov 2026).
  • Sell in atomic units (TAB) or packaging (STRIP).
  • Test AI Purchase OCR invoice upload.
